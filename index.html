Yijun Liao

First-year Undergraduate · Chongqing University of Technology

My research interests lie in understanding how language models learn, adapt, and make decisions. I work across LLM agents, reinforcement learning and alignment, and mechanistic interpretability, with an emphasis on controlled experiments, causal analysis, and rigorous evaluation. I am especially interested in the gap between what a model achieves and the mechanisms or strategies that produce it: strong task performance alone neither establishes robust generalization nor explains how a model reaches its decisions.

Preprint

Shortcut Before Circuit: Document Statistics Time In-Context Conflict Resolution

Yijun Liao, Fanwei Liang

Under review, 2026

We train 26M-parameter transformers on a synthetic language where recency and rarity always co-occur, then pull the two cues apart with a minimal edit. Models reach near-perfect accuracy, yet their behavior under intervention is inconsistent. In our controlled setting, document statistics influence when models move beyond positional shortcuts, while the mechanisms that emerge vary across random seeds.

Preprint

Overcoming State Inertia: Minimally Invasive Temporal Alignment for Evolving Contexts

Yijun Liao

arXiv preprint, 2025

Long-context dialogue models cling to stale history and fail to follow shifting user intent, while existing fixes erode general language ability. We propose DZ-TiDPO, combining conflict-aware preference optimization with a temporal attention bias that decouples state updates from language modeling, and introduce IC-Bench for evaluation.
